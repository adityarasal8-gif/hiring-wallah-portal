About the PM Internship Portal

Hiring Wallah is inspired by the mandate of the Prime Minister's Office to bridge the gap between youth from regional and rural areas and verified work opportunities. We leverage simple, light-weight AI to provide transparent and equitable access.

  • ✅ **Verified Opportunities:** Only approved government and authorized private sector internships are listed.
  • ✅ **Focus on Youth:** Built for first-time internship seekers and those with low digital literacy.
  • ✅ **National Reach:** Opportunities available across all major cities and regional districts.
